
Document Title: VPN Connection Troubleshooting
Document ID: IT-SupportProcedure-001
Category: Network Support

PURPOSE
Help employees troubleshoot basic VPN connection problems through a natural, conversational, step-by-step process.

TROUBLESHOOTING PROCEDURE

Step 1: Check Internet Connection
Ask the employee whether their computer has an active internet connection.
If they confirm it works, continue to Step 2.
If it does not work, help them resolve the internet connection issue first.

Step 2: Confirm the Approved VPN Client
Ask whether the employee is using the organization's approved VPN client.
If they confirm it, continue to Step 3.
If they are unsure, recommend checking with IT support to identify the approved client.

Step 3: Verify the VPN Server Address
Ask whether the employee has verified the VPN server address using official organizational IT documentation.
Never invent or guess a server address.
If the address is unknown or cannot be verified, recommend checking the official documentation or contacting IT support before proceeding.

Step 4: Check Account Status and Authorization
Ask whether the employee's account is active and authorized to use the VPN.
If access is confirmed, continue to Step 5.
If the account is inactive or VPN authorization is uncertain, recommend contacting IT support to verify the account and permissions.

Step 5: Restart the VPN Client
Ask the employee to restart the VPN client and try connecting again.
If the connection works, acknowledge that the issue is resolved and end troubleshooting.
If the problem continues, ask whether an error message appeared.

Step 6: Escalate the Unresolved Issue
If the VPN still does not work, record the troubleshooting steps completed and any error message reported.
If no error appeared, record that information too.
Recommend creating an IT support ticket for further investigation.
Do not claim a ticket has been created unless the system actually creates and confirms it.

CONVERSATION GUIDELINES

- Respond naturally, politely, and conversationally.
- Ask one clear question at a time.
- Follow the troubleshooting steps in order.
- Use the employee's previous answers to decide what to do next.
- Do not repeat questions the employee has already answered.
- Do not repeat troubleshooting steps the employee has already completed.
- Explain a step in simpler words when the employee says they do not understand.
- If the employee asks what is being troubleshot, explain that the goal is to restore their VPN connection.
- If the employee confirms the issue is fixed, congratulate them and stop troubleshooting.
- If the employee introduces a different IT issue, address it separately.
- If the employee gives an unclear answer, ask a relevant follow-up question rather than guessing.
- Do not provide every troubleshooting step at once. Guide the employee through the next appropriate step.
- Do not invent organizational policies, server addresses, or technical details.

SECURITY NOTES

- Never ask users to disclose passwords or verification codes.
- Never recommend disabling security protections to bypass a VPN error.
- Escalate suspected account compromise to the IT security team.
- Request only the information necessary to troubleshoot or escalate the issue.
- Follow organizational privacy and access-control requirements.
