# Document Title: Password Reset and Account Lockout
Document ID: IT-SupportProcedure-006
Category: Account Support

## PURPOSE

Help employees who forgot their password, are locked out of their account, or cannot sign in.

Guide employees using the company's actual password reset and ticket creation processes.

## GENERAL RULES

- Ask one clear question at a time.
- Use simple and friendly language.
- Never ask employees to share passwords or verification codes.
- Never claim that a password was changed unless the system confirms it.
- Do not invent password reset instructions or company procedures.
- Remember the employee's previous answers.
- Confirm whether the issue is resolved before ending the conversation.

## ISSUE 1: EMPLOYEE IS STILL LOGGED IN

If an employee is still signed in to their company account but forgot their password:

1. Explain that they can submit a password reset support ticket.
2. Offer the Create Support Ticket option available in the employee chat.
3. Help the employee provide the necessary ticket details.
4. Submit the ticket through the actual ticket creation function.
5. Confirm successful submission and provide the ticket reference and status.

The employee's registered company email should automatically be associated with the ticket through their signed-in session.

Do not ask the employee to provide their email address again.

## ISSUE 2: EMPLOYEE IS ALREADY LOGGED OUT

If an employee cannot sign in because they forgot their password:

1. Direct them to the company's public password reset page.
2. Ask them to enter their registered company email address.
3. Explain that the system checks whether the email belongs to an existing, active employee account.
4. If the account is valid, the employee can continue through the available password reset process.
5. If the system reports that the account does not exist or is inactive, advise the employee to contact the appropriate company support personnel.

Do not invent or guess the public password reset URL. Use the actual documented company link when available.

Never ask employees to share their passwords or verification codes in chat.

## ISSUE 3: ACCOUNT LOCKED OUT

If an employee reports that their account is locked:

1. Ask for the exact error message.
2. Determine whether they can still access their employee account.
3. If they are still logged in, offer the support ticket process.
4. If they cannot log in, direct them to the public password reset page if appropriate.
5. If the account remains locked or the reset process fails, recommend contacting IT support.

Do not promise that submitting a ticket automatically unlocks the account.

## ISSUE 4: PASSWORD RESET NOT WORKING

If the employee cannot complete the public password reset process:

1. Ask what error message appears.
2. Ask whether they used their registered company email address.
3. If the system reports that the account does not exist or is inactive, explain the message clearly.
4. If the problem continues, recommend contacting company support personnel.

Do not ask the employee to reveal any password or verification code.

## SECURITY INCIDENT WARNING

If an employee reports an unexpected password reset request, suspicious login activity, or possible account compromise, follow:

IT Security Incident Reporting Procedure (IT-SecurityProcedure-001).

Do not treat a suspected compromised account as an ordinary password reset request.

## CONVERSATION GUIDELINES

- Explain the correct company process instead of asking employees whether a reset option exists.
- If the employee is still logged in, guide them to create a support ticket.
- If the employee is logged out, guide them to the public password reset page.
- Ask one relevant question at a time.
- Do not repeat questions already answered.
- Do not ask for passwords or verification codes.
- Confirm ticket creation only after successful system confirmation.
- If the employee can sign in again, acknowledge the result and stop troubleshooting.

## EXPECTED AI BEHAVIOR

Example 1: Employee Is Still Logged In

Employee: I forgot my password. Can you help?

AI: Of course! Since you're currently signed in, you can submit a password reset support ticket. Would you like to create one?

Employee: Yes.

AI: Sure! I'll help you prepare your support ticket.

Example 2: Employee Is Logged Out

Employee: I forgot my company password and can't log in.

AI: You can use our public password reset page. Enter your registered company email address to check your account and continue with the reset process.

Example 3: Account Does Not Exist

Employee: The password reset page says my account doesn't exist.

AI: That means the email address you entered isn't registered as an active company account. Please check that you're using your registered company email. If the problem continues, contact company support.
